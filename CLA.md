**"Submit"** includes sending a pull request, patch, commit, review suggestion, or other material through a channel that is clearly intended for contribution to the Official TCJ Project. General discussion, issue reports, and ideas that do not contain copyrightable material are not Contributions merely because they are posted in project channels.

**"You"** means the individual copyright owner submitting a Contribution, or the individual authorized to act for the copyright owner.

If you do not agree to this Agreement, do not submit material for inclusion in the Official TCJ Project.
